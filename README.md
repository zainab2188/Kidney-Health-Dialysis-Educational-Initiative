# Kidney Health & Dialysis Educational Initiative

## Overview

This project documents an Arabic kidney health and dialysis educational initiative developed as a volunteer awareness activity.

The initiative consisted of an 8-page educational booklet titled "الفشل الكلوي والديلزة", accompanied by a pre/post reading questionnaire.

The project was inspired by my experience as a Dialysis Nursing student and my interest in making kidney health information easier to understand and more accessible to patients, families, and the wider community.

The questionnaire received 15 completed responses. The results are presented descriptively and are not intended to establish a causal effect or represent the wider population.

## Objectives

The initiative aimed to:

- Present basic information about kidney function and kidney failure in simple Arabic.
- Explain common causes, symptoms, diagnostic tests, and treatment options.
- Introduce basic information about dialysis and kidney transplantation.
- Provide an accessible educational resource for non-specialist readers.
- Explore participants' prior knowledge and their responses after reading the booklet.

Title: الفشل الكلوي والديلزة

- Kidney functions

- Signs and symptoms
- Diagnostic tests
- Dialysis
- Kidney transplantation
- Basic health awareness and early monitoring

## Questionnaire

A short pre/post reading questionnaire was used to explore participants' prior knowledge and their responses after reading the booklet.

- Completed responses: 15
- Format: Pre-reading and post-reading questions
- Purpose: Descriptive exploration of participants' knowledge and responses

No personally identifying participant information is presented in this repository.

The pre- and post-reading sections used different questions. Therefore, the results should not be interpreted as a controlled measurement of individual learning gain or as proof of a causal effect of the booklet.

## Survey Results

### Before Reading

Among the 15 completed responses:

- 12/15 (80%) correctly identified filtering waste and excess substances from the blood as a primary kidney function.
- 8/15 (53.3%) recognized that kidney disease may not show clear symptoms in its early stages.
- 11/15 (73.3%) identified diabetes and high blood pressure as common causes associated with kidney failure.
- 12/15 (80%) correctly understood that dialysis does not restore the kidneys to their normal function.
- 10/15 (33.3%) already knew that the kidneys play roles in bone health, vitamin D activation, and red blood cell production, while **5/15 (66.7%)** were not aware of these functions.

### After Reading

Among the 15 completed responses:

- 15/15 (100%) identified blood and urine tests as important tests for checking kidney health.
- 14/15 (93.3%) selected the expected answer regarding the behavioral factors covered by the questionnaire.
- 14/15 (93.3%) identified kidney transplantation as the answer to the treatment question presented in the questionnaire.
- 15/15 (100%) identified regular blood, urine, and blood-pressure checks as an early approach to monitoring kidney health.
- 14/15 (93.3%) reported that the booklet's information was very clear and easy to understand.

These results provide a descriptive summary of the responses collected for this initiative.

Because the pre- and post-reading questions were not identical, the results should not be interpreted as proof of a causal improvement in knowledge.
